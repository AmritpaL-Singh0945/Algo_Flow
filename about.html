The Vision

AlgoFlow demystifies complex data structures and algorithms through fluid, 60FPS visualizations. No black magic, just pure execution steps. It was built to push the boundaries of pure Vanilla JavaScript, exploring raw DOM manipulation, Web Workers, and Generator functions without relying on heavy frontend frameworks.

The Creator

Developed by Amritpal Singh, currently pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science at Chitkara University (Class of 2028). While typically working with full-stack frameworks like React and Django, this project served as a deep dive into core web technologies and algorithm optimization.
